EVIDENCE GATHERED: (1) Writer's block research shows inner critic is major blocker - 'that inner critic that says This is junk' - suggesting feedback tools need careful positioning. (2) Feedback pain point validated: 'Everyone is a music critic—not everyone's critique is valuable' - users struggle to find QUALITY feedback. (3) Competitor gap: Most tools (LyricStudio, Jarvis, Hookpad) focus on generation, NOT critique/feedback. RapPad mentions feedback but rap-specific. No major player does AI critique well. (4) Market opportunity: Differentiation is clear - feedback-first fills a gap. CONCERNS: (1) Risk of triggering inner critic if feedback is too harsh. (2) Must nail configurable feedback styles to avoid discouraging users.
